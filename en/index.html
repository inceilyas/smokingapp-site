Breathe In

Track the time since you quit smoking, the money in your pocket and the cigarettes you did not smoke, in a calm interface that never blames you.

For iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Support Privacy Policy

Not medical advice. Breathe In is a tracking and motivation tool. It does not diagnose, it does not recommend treatment and it is not a medical device. For decisions about your health, talk to a health professional.

What it does

  • Time smoke-free

    A counter that runs in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds from the moment you quit.

  • Money saved

    Calculated from your daily cigarettes, pack price and cigarettes per pack. If the price changes, the counter continues at the new price.

  • Cigarettes not smoked and time saved

    How many cigarettes you have not smoked since quitting, and the time you would have spent on them.

  • Health timeline based on WHO

    See the benefits of quitting published by the World Health Organization on a timeline tied to your counter. It is not a personal medical assessment.

  • A supportive "I smoked" flow

    If you slip, there is no blame. You are reminded of what you have achieved, then it is up to you to reset the counter or continue.

  • Notifications

    Local notifications for recovery stages, milestones and a daily boost of motivation. You can turn categories off and hide details in notifications.

  • Friends leaderboard

    With an optional account, compare points, time and streak with your friends. Points are based on your smoke-free day streak.

  • Watcher

    A person you trust can follow your progress and gets a notification when you reset or choose to continue after a slip. Being a watcher is free and is always set up with your approval.

Works without an account

The counter, metrics, timeline, slip flow and past attempts work without an account and your data stays on your device. An account (sign in with Apple) is only needed for the friends leaderboard and the watcher feature. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Free and Premium

Core tracking is free and fully functional. Premium unlocks the friends leaderboard, the watcher feature, home screen visualizations and personal health thresholds. The watched person pays; watchers are free. Options are a monthly subscription, a 3-month subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase, all through your Apple account. Details are in the Terms of Use.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads, no advertising identifier, no third-party analytics and no tracking. Your data is never sold.
